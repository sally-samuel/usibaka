About us

An oil and gas company built around the full value chain

Usibaka Oil and Gas Limited is based in Ikoyi, Lagos, Nigeria. We explore for petroleum, harness and process natural gas, and supply crude, condensates, refined fuels and gas to industry and households.

Alongside production and trading, we deliver drilling, well services, offshore support and engineering, procurement and construction, so a project can be handled by one team from the first well to the final delivery.

  • UpstreamPetroleum prospecting, gas processing and turnkey well construction.
  • ServicesDrilling rigs, well services, ROV operations and offshore support.
  • TradingCrude, condensates, fuels, lubricants and gas, with depots and storage.
What we do

Our services

From the first well to the final delivery, we cover the oil and gas value chain. Choose a service to see what it includes.

Exploration & Production

We prospect for petroleum, then harness, process and supply natural gas.

  • Petroleum prospecting
  • Natural gas harnessing, processing and supply
  • Turnkey well construction
  • Production processing (EPC basis)
  • Early production equipment
  • Modular gas processing units
  • Wells, rigs and refinery construction
  • Pipeline laying for oil and gas supplies

Drilling & Well Services

Rigs, crews and well services for major players, on land and offshore.

  • Land and jack-up drilling rigs
  • Drilling and production supplies and consumables
  • Slick line services
  • Down-hole well services
  • ROV operations
  • SURF (subsea umbilicals, risers and flowlines)
  • Accommodation rigs and jack-up barges
  • Supply vessels and marine operations

Crude Sale

  • Crude oil marketing
  • Supply and distribution
  • Dealing as oil and gas merchant

Condensates

We produce, process and supply condensates alongside our gas operations.

  • Gas and condensate processing
  • Treatment and refining of produce
  • Supply to industrial buyers

Fuel Supply & Trading

Refined products and gas for industry, homes and kitchens.

  • Petrol, kerosene and diesel

  • Industrial, domestic and cooking gas

  • Depots and storage

Engineering & Technical Solutions

Engineering, procurement and construction under one roof.
